Privacy Policy

IDSnap helps you create passport, visa, and ID photos on your iPhone. This page explains what the app does with the photos and settings you provide, and what the ad and purchase services it uses receive.

Updated November 16, 2026

Photos you choose

The app receives a photo only when you take one with the camera or choose one using the iOS photo picker. It processes the image on your iPhone to find a face, show alignment guidance, check photo quality, adjust the crop, optionally replace the background for sizes that allow it, and create a digital photo or print sheet. Your photos are never uploaded, to us or to anyone else.

Storage on your iPhone

When you save a finished photo, IDSnap keeps the original image, the finished image, and its crop and size details in the app's own storage, so you can reopen it in My Photos. Your settings, such as the default size, output choices, and camera options, and a count of the ads you have watched, are kept in iOS preferences.

One record is kept in the iOS keychain: whether this iPhone has used its free photo. It holds only a number, contains no photo or personal details, and is not synced to iCloud Keychain. iOS keeps keychain items when an app is removed, so reinstalling IDSnap does not give a second free photo. Like other app data, it can be included in your encrypted or iCloud device backups.

Camera and Photos access

The app asks for camera access when you use the camera. The system photo picker lets you select an image without giving the app access to the rest of your photo library. When you save an output to Photos, iOS asks for permission to add photos. You can change camera and Photos permissions in iPhone Settings.

Saving and sharing

Saving to Photos creates a separate copy in your photo library. Sharing uses the iOS share sheet and the destination you select. Copies outside IDSnap are managed by those destinations and their privacy practices.

Data we collect

None. IDSnap has no account and no server, and the app itself runs no analytics. If you choose to share app analytics and crash reports with developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us anonymous usage statistics and crash reports.

Ads

Your first photo saves free. After that, each new photo can be saved by watching one ad instead of buying Full access. Those ads come from Google Mobile Ads. To serve and measure them, Google may collect your device's identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions, and performance and crash diagnostics. Ads are personalized only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks before any ad, and Ad privacy choices in the app's Settings changes your answer.

None of this starts until you first choose to watch an ad, and your photos are never shared with Google. Google is the only third party that receives data from the app, and it protects that data to a standard at least equal to this policy, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

The App Store handles the one-time Full access purchase, which can be shared through Family Sharing. The app checks the purchase on your iPhone and never sees your payment details. With Full access, the app requests no ads.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Saved photos: delete them in My Photos, and they are removed from the app's storage. Copies you saved to Photos are managed in the Photos app.
  • Everything else in the app: removing IDSnap deletes its storage and preferences from your iPhone. Only the free-photo record described above stays in the keychain.
  • Tracking: turn it on or off at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad consent: where Google's consent form applies, change your answer in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Data held by Google: see My Ad Center and Google's Privacy Policy for its controls and deletion requests.

We keep no data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete. If you have a question, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

When the app's handling of data changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
