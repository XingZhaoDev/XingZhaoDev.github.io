Support

Answers to common IDSnap questions. If your issue isn't covered here, email us and we'll help.

Updated November 16, 2026

The camera is unavailable

Open iPhone Settings, find IDSnap, and allow Camera access. Return to the app and try again. You can also choose a portrait from your photo library.

No face is found

Try a clear photo with one person, even lighting, and an unobstructed face. Move closer or choose another image if the face is too small or blurry.

I cannot change the background

Background replacement is available only for sizes that allow it. For other sizes, use a photo taken against the required plain background. Always check the official requirements for the destination accepting your photo.

A photo will not save

Check that your iPhone has free storage and that IDSnap can add photos in Settings. Then try saving again. If the app says the photo was saved to Photos but did not appear in My Photos, check the iOS Photos app for the saved copy.

Saving with an ad

Your first photo saves free. After that, each new photo can be saved by watching one short ad, or every photo can be saved without ads by buying Full access once. If you close an ad before it ends, the photo isn't saved, so tap Save and try again. If no ad can load, for example while you're offline, the photo saves anyway.

Full access is missing

Sign in with the Apple Account you used to buy it, then tap Restore purchases in the app's Settings. Family members can use it too when Family Sharing is on. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem page.

Will my photo be accepted?

IDSnap provides size and alignment guidance, but acceptance is decided by the organization receiving the photo. Review its latest official requirements before submitting or printing.
